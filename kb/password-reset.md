# Resetting your password

If you forgot your password, open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter the email address on your account and we will send a reset link.

The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If it has expired, request a new one. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within five minutes.

# Locked out of your account

After five failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait for the lock to clear, then sign in again or reset your password.
